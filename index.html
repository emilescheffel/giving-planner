How the tax credits are calculated

Both credits are progressive: the first dollars are credited most generously, later dollars less so. The planner applies these brackets to your combined annual total at each level, then caps the result. Because the top of each scale is reached well below the annual contribution limit, giving more than the amounts below returns no extra credit.
